About Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor and electrical engineer known for revolutionizing the way energy is generated and transmitted. His work on alternating current (AC), radio, X-rays, and wireless power has left an enduring mark on science and technology.

Fast facts

  • Born: July 10, 1856
  • Died: January 7, 1943
  • Best known for: AC electrical systems, radio, induction motor
  • Nickname: "The Master of Lightning"

Legacy of Innovation

Alternating Current

Tesla's AC system proved superior for long-distance transmission and became the foundation of modern electrical grids.

Wireless Energy

He dreamed of transmitting power without wires, and his experiments with Tesla coils laid the groundwork for modern wireless technologies.

Radio & Communication

Tesla demonstrated radio wave transmission before many contemporaries, influencing the future of global communication.

Milestones

1884

Arrived in New York and began working with Thomas Edison, shortly after proposing the AC current system.

1891

Invented the Tesla coil and became a U.S. citizen, demonstrating wireless transmission of energy.

1893

Powered the World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago with AC lighting, showcasing the safety and strength of his system.

1917

Developed concepts for remote control and guided missiles, highlighting his forward-thinking vision.
